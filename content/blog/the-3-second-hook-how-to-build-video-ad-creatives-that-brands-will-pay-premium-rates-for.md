## 🚀 3 High-Converting Video Hook Frameworks to Script

Stop starting your video pitches with: *"Hey guys, today I am reviewing this product!"* This standard introduction guarantees an immediate user scroll. Instead, script your video assets using these three validated direct-response frameworks:

### Framework 1: The Visual Pattern Interrupt
*   **The Concept:** Start the video in the middle of an action or use unexpected visual positioning to jar the viewer out of their passive scrolling trance.
*   **Text Overlay Examples:** 
    *   *"Stop scrolling if you still use standard makeup brushes."*
    *   *"I was today years old when I found out this bedroom hack existed."*
*   **Visual Action:** Begin with a tight macro close-up of a product texture, an expressive facial reaction, or a fast-paced movement directly toward the camera lens.

### Framework 2: The Negative Emotion / Problem Trigger
*   **The Concept:** Lead directly with a common, painful frustration your target audience experiences on a daily basis, then position the brand's product as the absolute relief solution.
*   **Text Overlay Examples:** 
    *   *"This is exactly why your current skincare routine is causing breakouts."*
    *   *"Unpopular opinion: Most remote work desk setups are ruining your posture."*
*   **Visual Action:** Show a clear, relatable visual display of the frustration (e.g., looking stressed at a messy workspace or pointing out uneven skin texture under natural lighting).

### Framework 3: The Direct Analytical Proof
*   **The Concept:** Lead with a bold, undeniable data point, financial metric, or extreme transformation result to establish instant psychological authority.
*   **Text Overlay Examples:** 
    *   *"How this one simple desktop application saved me 12 hours of manual work this week."*
    *   *"The exact routine I used to fix my hyperpigmentation in less than 30 days."*
*   **Visual Action:** Hold up a physical transformation asset, overlay a clean data chart graphic element, or show a clear before-and-after split-screen frame.
