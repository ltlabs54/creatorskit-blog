Finding active brand budgets is the biggest challenge for UGC creators. While many brands are running ads, securing paid deals requires a professional approach, personalized pitching, and a solid portfolio. This guide provides a curated list of 100 brands, proven outreach scripts, and strategies to land paid partnerships

The 6-Step Brand Pitch Framework That Converts to convert cold outreach into paid contracts, follow this framework to stand out to brand managers:

1. **Execute Granular Brand Research:**

    Review the target brand’s TikTok and Instagram ad feeds to understand their aesthetic.
2. **Name-Drop High-Intent Product Lines:**

    Reference specific items you want to showcase, rather than generic categories.
3. **Enforce the 5-Sentence Sweet Spot:**

    Keep emails concise, 3 to 5 sentences max.

**Step 1: Execute Granular Competitor Research**

Never send a mass copy-and-paste email message. Spend 10 minutes reviewing the target brand’s TikTok and Instagram ad feeds. Identify if they use authentic unboxings, quick problem-solution hooks, or aesthetic routines.

**Step 2: Name-Drop High-Intent Product Lines**

Generic pitches say: "I want to collaborate with your brand!".High-converting pitches call out a specific item:

❌ "I would love to work with Glossier to review makeup items!"

✅ "I’ve been tracking Glossier’s Cloud Paint in Puff for my everyday minimal routine. I want to build a high-retention video showcasing how it holds under direct studio lighting."

**Step 3: Enforce the 5-Sentence Sweet Spot**

Marketing directors delete long introductory blocks. Keep your text capped at 3 to 5 sentences max. Clearly define your content niche, share a verified view or conversion metric, and present a direct call to action (CTA).

**Step 4: Leading with Value Propositions**

Brands don’t buy your goals; they buy solutions to their marketing gaps. Frame your pitch around driving conversions, expanding ad click-through rates (CTR), or capturing specific demographics.

**Step 5: Embed a Live Responsive Link**

A professional, standalone portfolio link is non-negotiable. Stop dropping messy Google Drive folders or flat PDF attachments that fail to load on mobile screens. Marketing managers need to see crisp video rendering and verified analytics instantly

**Step 6: Grant Explicit Advertising Usage Rights**

When pitching, mention that your content deliverables include ad usage rights. Brands actively search for creators who understand paid media rights, and this justification allows you to charge premium rates upfront.
