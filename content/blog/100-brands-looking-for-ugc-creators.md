Discover 100 brands actively working with UGC creators. This living list includes outreach templates, portfolio tips, and the common mistakes that get creators ignored.

## Why a target list beats spray-and-pray

Sending the same DM to 500 brands feels productive. It isn't. A focused list of brands that *already* buy UGC converts far better, because you're pitching demand that exists.

## How to use this list

- Filter for brands in your niche.
- Personalize the first line of every pitch.
- Track each one in a CRM so no follow-up slips.

## Outreach template

> Hi [Name], I create short-form UGC for [category] brands. I made a 15-second concept for [Product] — mind if I send it over?

Keep it short. Lead with value, not your life story.
