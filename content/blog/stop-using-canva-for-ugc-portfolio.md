Canva is great for design, but static PDFs are quietly killing your brand deals. Discover why brands prefer live, data-rich portfolios with real-time metrics.

## The problem with the PDF portfolio

A PDF is a snapshot. By the time a brand opens it, your best-performing video already has 3x the views. Static assets can't show momentum.

## What brands actually want to see

- Live view/engagement counts
- Clickable links to the real posts
- Clear rates and turnaround times

## The fix

Use a live portfolio link that updates itself. It signals you treat this like a business — and that's exactly the creator brands want to pay.
